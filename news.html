How this page is built

Every morning an automated job reads the official feeds — immigration departments, visa bulletins, civil aviation notices and mainstream reporting — filters out anything that doesn't affect an applicant from India, and publishes what's left here with a link to the original.

We summarise. We do not rewrite the rule. Where a decision matters to your case, open the source link and read it, or call us and we'll read it with you.

What we deliberately leave out

  • Fraud and scam allegations naming individuals or firms
  • Agent advertisements dressed up as news
  • Anything promising guaranteed visas or shortcuts
  • Unverified forum claims with no official source
