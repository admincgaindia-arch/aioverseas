How to read this

Four things decide most destination choices, and they are the four filters above.

  • TimelineThe Gulf moves in weeks, Canada and New Zealand in months, the US on whenever an interview slot opens. If you have a fixed intake or joining date, this filter matters more than any other.
  • English testStudy and most skilled routes need one. Visitor, business and several employment routes do not. Filtering by this stops you paying for a test you never needed.
  • Stay-backPermission to work after your course without a sponsor. For most study applicants this is the real reason to pick one country over another — not the tuition difference.
  • What is missing on purposeFees. Government fees change several times a year and our fee depends on the case. You get both in writing after the free assessment, separated line by line.

About this tool

What it does, and what it deliberately does not do.

Why does this tool not show fees?

Because government fees change several times a year and our professional fee depends on the case. A number published today would be wrong within months, and a wrong number on a visa website costs somebody real money. You get the full cost in writing after the free assessment, split into our fee and third-party fees.

Are these timelines guaranteed?

No. They are the published indicative ranges for each route. Seasonal load, document queries and interview availability all move them, and the visa office decides, not us.

What does stay-back mean?

Permission to remain and work in the country after finishing your course, without a new employer sponsoring you first. Canada's PGWP, the UK Graduate route, Australia's 485 and Ireland's Stamp 1G are the main ones. It is usually the single biggest factor in a study destination decision.

The route I want is not listed. Do you still handle it?

Possibly. This tool covers the eight destinations we file for regularly. We handle other countries case by case — call and ask.

Filtered it down and still unsure?

That is normal — a filter narrows options, it does not read your file. Send us your marks, funds position and timeline and we will tell you which of these routes actually fits.
