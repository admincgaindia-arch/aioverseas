Why United States files actually get refused

Not theory. These are the failure points we see repeatedly on this route — and every one of them is fixable before you file.

  • Answers that do not match the fileThe officer compares what you say to what you filed. Rehearsed lines that contradict your documents end the interview.
  • Unclear source of fundingWho is paying, from what income, and why. If you cannot answer plainly, the file fails.
  • No credible reason to returnSection 214(b) refusals turn on this. Document your ties rather than assuming they are obvious.
  • Course chosen for the visa, not the careerIf the programme does not fit your background, the officer will say so.

United States questions

If yours is not here, call and ask it. The first conversation costs nothing.

Why do US visas get refused so often?

Most refusals are under section 214(b) — the officer was not satisfied you would return. It is a judgement made in a short interview, which is why preparation matters more here than paperwork volume.

Can I reapply after a refusal?

Yes, and there is no fixed waiting period. But reapplying without changing anything usually produces the same result. We work out what was not satisfied first.

What is SEVIS?

The student tracking system. You pay the SEVIS fee after receiving your I-20 and before the visa interview, and you must carry the receipt.
