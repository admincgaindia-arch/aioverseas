How to use this page

Three things worth knowing before you plan around any date here.

  • Work backwards, not forwardsTake your intake date, subtract the processing range, subtract four weeks for building the file, subtract the time to season funds. That is your real start date — usually earlier than people expect.
  • Filing cutoffs beat application deadlinesA university may accept your application in December. That does not mean a visa filed in December will decide by January. We show the date that actually governs the outcome.
  • Tags are not decorationConfirmed comes from a government source. Reported comes from press. Indicative is a typical window. Never book a flight or pay a deposit against an indicative date.

About this calendar

What it covers and where the dates come from.

How exact are these dates?

Each item is tagged. Confirmed means the date comes from a government source. Reported means it has been reported by mainstream press but we have not seen the notification ourselves. Indicative means it is a typical window, not a fixed date — university deadlines in particular vary by institution and course.

Why do you show a filing cutoff that is not an official deadline?

Because the official deadline is not the one that matters. If processing runs twelve weeks and your course starts in January, the date that decides your outcome is when you lodge, not when the university stops accepting applications. We show both.

What if I miss a window?

There is almost always a next intake — Canada has January, May and September, Australia has February and July, the UK has September and January. Missing one is a delay, not an ending. Rushing a weak file to catch a window is what actually costs people a year.

Do you track these for your clients?

Yes. Once we take a file, the intake dates, document deadlines and appointment windows for that case are ours to watch, not yours.

Which of these dates is yours?

Tell us your target intake and we will work backwards to the date you actually need to start — and tell you plainly if it has already passed for this cycle.
