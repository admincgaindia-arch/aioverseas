Canada

The study-to-PR country

Canada remains the most complete route for Indian applicants: a study permit that leads to a post-graduation work permit, work experience that earns points, and a permanent residence system that rewards that experience. It is also the country where financial documentation is scrutinised hardest.

Why Canada files actually get refused

Not theory. These are the failure points we see repeatedly on this route — and every one of them is fixable before you file.

  • Funds that appeared recentlyCanadian officers read the statement pattern, not just the closing balance. Season your funds for months.
  • A course that does not follow your historyA sharp switch needs a written, documented explanation, not silence.
  • Weak ties to IndiaEven on a study permit the officer asks whether you will leave when the permit ends.
  • Undisclosed refusal historyCanada shares refusal data with several countries. Disclose it and address it.

Canada questions

If yours is not here, call and ask it. The first conversation costs nothing.

Does a study permit guarantee PR?

No. It creates a pathway — study, then a post-graduation work permit, then skilled experience that earns points. Each stage has its own requirements and none of them is automatic.

What is a GIC?

A Guaranteed Investment Certificate. You deposit your first year of living costs into a Canadian account before filing, and it is released to you in monthly instalments after you land.

Can my spouse work while I study?

In many cases yes, through an open work permit, but eligibility depends on your programme level and current rules. We confirm this before you plan around it.
